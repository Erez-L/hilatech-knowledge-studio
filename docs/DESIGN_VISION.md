# Design vision

Status: implemented for the landing page and the Knowledge Workspace (Milestone 1). Other screens follow this document when they are built.

## The idea

HilaTech Knowledge Studio answers questions from a company's documents and shows where each answer came from. The design is built around that second half. A chat box is ordinary; an answer you can check is the product.

The direction is **editorial intelligence**: the calm of a well-set publication, applied to a working tool. The interface behaves like an annotated document. Answers are the text, citations are the footnotes, and the evidence panel is the margin where the source is laid open.

## The signature: the highlighter

One device carries the identity everywhere: **a passage marked in Signal Lime, the way a person marks a line with a highlighter.**

- In the evidence panel it marks the exact passage an answer relies on.
- In the hero it marks the two passages behind a sample answer, and a thin thread runs from each citation to its page.
- In the logo it is the one coloured line on a small page.
- On hover it sweeps across example questions, in the reading direction.

Lime therefore always means "this is evidence, or this is the action that produces it" (the send button, a selected citation). It is not used for decoration, backgrounds or generic emphasis.

## Principles

1. **Evidence is part of the layout, not an attachment.** Sources get their own region with a real document viewer. It takes no width until there is something to show.
2. **Paper and interface are different materials.** Document pages have nearly square corners, a hairline border and a soft shadow. Interface surfaces are softer and rounder. A reader can tell at a glance what is quoted from a document and what the product wrote.
3. **Type does the work.** Large, tight headlines; comfortable body text; a mono face only for facts about documents (page numbers, file names, keyboard keys). No decorative labels above headings.
4. **Quiet by default, one moment of motion.** The page-load moment is the hero's threads drawing and highlights sweeping in. Everything else moves only in response to the person.
5. **Honest states.** Anything that is not live is labelled where it appears. The workspace says, on screen, that its answers are pre-written samples until a model is connected.
6. **Hebrew is a first language here, not a mirror.** Layout uses logical properties throughout, Hebrew has its own line heights and tracking, and brand graphics do not flip.

## What it deliberately is not

- Not a dashboard: no KPI tiles, charts or metric cards.
- Not a chat clone: the conversation shares the screen with sources and a document viewer.
- Not a card grid: sections use rules, columns, rows and one framed product stage.
- Not a gradient AI page: there are no gradients except the highlight itself and a faint ruled-paper ground.

## Voice

Plain and specific. Buttons say what happens ("Request a demo", "Send question"). Errors say what went wrong and what to do. The product never claims certifications, customers or numbers it does not have. Northstar Business Services and its documents are stated to be fictional.

## Light and dark

Light is porcelain paper with deep ink text. Dark is designed separately as a reading room: a green-black ground, lit paper surfaces instead of inverted white, lime promoted to the primary action colour, and translucent highlights so marked text stays readable.
